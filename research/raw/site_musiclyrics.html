Dynamic Lyrics

Lock Screen Lyrics

Elegantly display real-time lyrics on the lock screen for a complete music experience. Supports various customization styles, perfectly integrated with system design.

Dynamic Island Lyrics

Fully utilize the Dynamic Island space to display real-time lyrics of the currently playing song. Perfectly integrated with the system without affecting other notifications.

Home Screen Widgets

Beautiful home screen widgets with multiple sizes and styles to choose from. Integrate lyrics into your home screen and feel the rhythm of music anytime.

Floating Lyrics

Floating lyrics window with adjustable position, always stays on top without affecting other operations, supports various customizations.

Full Screen Lyrics

Immersive full-screen lyrics experience that clearly presents every line. Supports bilingual display for more focused music appreciation.

Lyrics Translation

Intelligent lyrics translation feature supporting multiple language translations. Helps you understand lyrics meaning and experience deeper charm of music.

StandBy Mode

Perfect support for iOS 17 StandBy mode, turning your device into a smart display. Automatically shows large-screen lyrics when device is lying flat.
