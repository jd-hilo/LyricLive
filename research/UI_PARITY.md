# UI parity — LyricsFlow screenshots vs LyricLive

Reference app: **LyricsFlow: Dynamic Lyrics**, App Store id `6779954248` (Kexin Li).
Our app: **LyricLive**. Same layouts and type scale; our own name, icon, and artwork.

Screenshots were pulled from the iTunes Lookup API on 2026-09-29 for storefronts `tw`, `hk`, `us`, and `my`, at 1290×2796. There is **no iPad screenshot set** and **no Apple TV set** on any of those storefronts. HK serves the same seven images as TW. MY serves the same images as US. The US/MY payload lists seven URLs, but positions 5 and 6 are the same file (`EN_Preview_5.jpg`), so the US set has six unique images and no separate landscape shot.

Files live in `research/screenshots/lyricsflow/`. They are reference only and are not in the app bundle. The red three-bar icon in `icon-reference-do-not-bundle.jpg` is theirs; LyricLive ships the speech-bubble icon generated by `scripts/make_icon.py`.

Frame of reference: 1290×2796 px ≈ 430×932 pt is the marketing canvas; the phone UI inside it is a 393×852 pt (iPhone 15/16 class) layout. Sizes below are points.

## Screen-by-screen

### Full-screen lyrics — `LyricsScreen`, `LyricsListView`, `LyricBackground`

Matched:

- Background is a slow-moving blur of several blobs taken from the album palette (`ArtworkProcessor` → `PaletteExtractor`), with a ~32% black veil. Styles in Settings: animated gradient (default), artwork blur, solid, black.
- Lines are left-aligned by default, horizontal padding 28 pt, 26 pt between groups.
- Current line: 34 pt bold (38 pt in landscape), white. Translation directly under it: 20 pt semibold (22 pt landscape), about 72% white.
- Other lines use the same face at 30–50% opacity, a slight scale (0.94) and up to ~2 pt of blur, animated with a spring (response 0.5, damping 0.85). The current line is scrolled to about 32% from the top.
- Dragging pauses auto-scroll for 3.5 s.
- Bottom card matches the screenshot bar: 44×44 art (radius 8), title 16 pt bold, artist 13 pt at 65% white, heart on the trailing edge. A scrubber and transport row sit in the same card so the screen is usable; the reference crop only shows the identity row.
- Landscape splits roughly 40/60: square art (max 200 pt, radius 16), title, prev / pause / next, then the lyric column.

Differences:

- The giant “歌詞翻譯” / “Lyric translation” words are marketing type drawn on top of the screenshot, not part of the app, so they are not in the UI.
- Their bottom bar has no scrubber in the crop. Ours adds a 5 pt scrubber and transport (translate, prev, pause, next, search) because a lyrics app needs them; the same controls appear on the competitor LyricFlow screen.
- Tap a line to seek. Apple Music and the demo source seek; Spotify seeks when the SDK is linked.

### Lock Screen — `LockScreenLyricView`

Matched:

- One card: 44 pt art, title 15 pt bold, artist 13 pt at 60% white, waveform glyph on the trailing side.
- Scrubber with elapsed time on the left and remaining time (`-2:48` style) on the right, 10 pt monospaced digits. While playing, the bar is a `ProgressView(timerInterval:)` so it moves without further updates.
- Transport centred under the scrubber: previous, pause, next, ~18 pt, semibold.
- Lyric block underneath on a lighter rounded rectangle (radius 16): previous line dim, current line 17 pt bold white, translation 13 pt at ~72%, next line at 40%. Centred.

Differences:

- Their crop also shows a star and an AirPlay glyph, which belong to the system Now Playing widget sitting above a lyric activity. A Live Activity cannot draw AirPlay output, and a favourite button needs an App Intent we did not add (favourite stays on the in-app heart).
- Their screenshot stacks under the system media widget. Ours is a single activity that includes art, transport, and lyrics, because the system widget is not ours to extend.

### Dynamic Island — `LyricLiveActivity`

Matched:

- Expanded: artwork leading, waveform trailing, previous line dim, current line ~19 pt bold with translation, next line dim, then previous / play / next.
- Compact: current line leading (one line, truncated), 22 pt art trailing.
- Minimal: artwork only.

Differences:

- Expanded height is whatever ActivityKit allocates; we cannot force their exact pill size.
- Compact text width is capped (~84 pt) so it fits the leading capsule.

### Home Screen widgets — `LyricWidget`, `NowPlayingWidget`

Matched:

- Small: art thumbnail, title, bold current line (up to 3 lines, 17 pt), dim next line.
- Medium: dark tinted card; left column has a music-note header, title, artist, 19 pt bold current line and either the translation or the dim next line; a vinyl (art in the label) hangs off the right edge.
- Large and extra-large: header vinyl (56 pt, larger on extra-large), title 16 pt bold, artist 13 pt at 60%, then a centred stack — previous at 30%, current extra-bold ~26 pt, next at 55% then 32%.
- Lock Screen accessory rectangular / inline / circular: current line, plus a circular progress gauge.
- Now Playing widget (their 1.1.0 addition): art plus previous / play / next via App Intents.

Differences:

- Widget backgrounds are a static gradient from the shared palette, not a live blur. Widgets cannot run the animated Canvas from the lyrics screen.
- Medium and large widgets, and Now Playing, show a lock state until Pro. Their own gating could not be observed without installing the app.
- The “iOS 27 extra-large” widget is `systemExtraLarge`, which WidgetKit already exposes.

### Landscape — `LyricsScreen.landscapeLayout`

Matched: left column art and three transport buttons, right column the same lyric list at a larger size, dark artwork-tinted background.

Difference: the column is a fixed 240 pt rather than a strict 40% on very wide iPads, so the art stays at most 200 pt.

### CarPlay — `CarPlayLyricsController`

Matched in content: current line marked as playing, previous and next lines as rows, title and artist as the section header, a second tab with play/pause, skip, and translation.

Differences, required by the platform:

- Audio-category CarPlay apps may only use list, grid, and now-playing templates. The dashboard split (map + lyric widget) and the two-column now-playing screen in screenshots 1 and 7 are custom-drawn marketing mockups. We cannot reproduce that chrome.
- Needs the `com.apple.developer.carplay-audio` entitlement, which is off until Apple grants it. See the README.

### Paywall — `PaywallView`

Their App Store listing (not a screenshot) sells three products. The paywall follows that shape:

| Their product | US price | LyricLive product id |
|---|---|---|
| Premium Yearly | $6.99 | `com.example.lyriclive.pro.yearly` |
| Premium Monthly | $2.99 | `com.example.lyriclive.pro.monthly` |
| Lifetime | $9.99 | `com.example.lyriclive.pro.lifetime` |

Yearly is badged “BEST VALUE” (or the computed savings). Restore, terms, and privacy sit under the purchase button, with the standard auto-renew disclosure. Prices come from StoreKit; `App/Resources/LyricLive.storekit` mirrors the US numbers for local testing.

### Onboarding, Home, Settings, share cards

These are not in their seven screenshots. They exist so the features those screenshots advertise can be turned on:

- Onboarding asks for Apple Music, the microphone (Shazam), and notifications / Live Activities.
- Home shows the source rows (Apple Music, Spotify, Shazam, demo) and shortcuts for the Live Activity, widgets, and CarPlay.
- Settings covers font size, alignment, font style, background, translation language, global offset, source toggles, and the background keep-alive.
- Share cards render selected lines over an artwork, midnight, sunrise, or paper background. Free cards carry a small wordmark; Pro removes it.

## What we deliberately did not copy

- The name LyricsFlow, the developer name, the red three-bar icon, and the Apple Music, Spotify, and Shazam logos. Sources are SF Symbols plus text.
- The demo song “N.I.G.H.T” / “Rose Moon” and its cover. LyricLive’s demo is “Midnight Signal” with art drawn in `DemoArtwork`.
- Screenshot marketing headlines (車載歌詞, 歌詞翻譯, 鎖定螢幕歌詞, and the English equivalents).
