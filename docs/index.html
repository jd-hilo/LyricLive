LyricLive

Time-synced lyrics for the song you are playing.

LyricLive shows lyrics that follow along with your music: full screen, on the Lock Screen, in the Dynamic Island, in Home Screen widgets, and in CarPlay. It works with Apple Music and Spotify, and can identify music playing nearby. Translation runs on your device.

Support

For help, bug reports, or refund questions, email jd@hilo.media. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, and the song you were playing.

Common questions

Lyrics are missing or out of sync. Lyrics come from LRCLIB, a community-run database. Some songs have no synced lyrics yet. You can adjust the timing offset in the lyrics screen.

How do I cancel my subscription? Open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose LyricLive. You can also go to Settings > Manage subscription inside the app. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged for the next period.

How do I restore a purchase? Open the LyricLive upgrade screen and tap Restore purchases while signed in with the same Apple ID.

Refunds. Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
