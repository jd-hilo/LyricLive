LyricLive Privacy Policy

Effective September 29, 2026

LyricLive is made by Hilo LLC ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the LyricLive iPhone app does with your information. The short version: LyricLive has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. We do not run servers that receive your data, and we do not sell or share personal information.

Information that stays on your device

LyricLive stores the following only on your iPhone, in the app's own storage: your settings, your favorite songs, cached lyrics, the song that is currently playing (so widgets, the Live Activity, and CarPlay can show it), and, if you connect Spotify, the sign-in token Spotify gives the app. Deleting the app deletes this data. You can also clear the lyrics cache in Settings.

Lyrics lookups

To find lyrics, the app sends the current song's title, artist, album, and length to LRCLIB, a free, community-run lyrics database. These requests do not include your name, Apple ID, contacts, location, or any advertising identifier. LRCLIB's own policies govern how it handles the requests it receives.

Apple Music and the Music app

If you allow access to Apple Music and your media library, LyricLive reads which song is playing and its playback position so it can sync the lyrics. It does not upload your library. Song lookups in the Apple Music catalog go to Apple and are covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Spotify

If you choose to connect Spotify, you sign in through the Spotify app. LyricLive then talks to the Spotify app on your device to learn which song is playing and to control playback. The sign-in token is stored on your device only. Your use of Spotify is covered by Spotify's Privacy Policy. You can switch LyricLive to another source at any time, and you can remove LyricLive's access from the Apps page of your Spotify account.

Microphone and song identification

Shazam mode is optional and only runs when you turn it on. It uses the microphone and Apple's ShazamKit to identify music playing around you. ShazamKit turns the audio into a signature on your device and sends that signature, not a recording, to Apple to find a match. LyricLive does not record, store, or upload audio.

Translation

Lyric translation uses Apple's Translation framework, which runs on your device (iOS 18 and later). Lyrics are not sent to us or to any translation service run by us. The first time you translate into a language, iOS may download that language from Apple.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime unlock are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. The app only receives confirmation from Apple of which purchase is active on your Apple ID.

Sharing

When you create a lyrics card, the image is made on your device. It is only shared if you choose to share it through the iOS share sheet.

Children

LyricLive is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Your choices

You can turn off Apple Music, microphone, and Live Activity access at any time in the iOS Settings app, remove LyricLive's access in your Spotify account, clear the lyrics cache in LyricLive's settings, or delete the app to remove all of its data. Because we do not hold personal data about you on our side, there is nothing for us to export or delete, but you are welcome to contact us with any request.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above. If a change affects how your information is handled in a significant way, we will also note it in the app's release notes.
